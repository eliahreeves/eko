<h1 align="center">eko</h1>

Social media used to be about connecting with friends. Now, it is about entertainment and selling ads.

eko attempts to restore the feeling of community to social media. With no algorithm and no ads, eko helps you stay connected with the people who matter most.

Express yourself through text, ascii art images, gifs, and polls. Whether you’re browsing posts from the global feed or just your inner circle, eko keeps social media simple.
